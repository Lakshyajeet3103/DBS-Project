# Your first week, made simple

Welcome to the team. Start your first week by setting up your workspace and meeting your team lead. Your buddy is your first point of contact for everyday questions.

## Your first-week checklist

Complete your profile, review the team handbook, and request access to the tools you need. Attend the team introduction on your first day and schedule a check-in with your buddy by Friday.

## Finding your footing

Use the document library to find approved guides. Ask the assistant a question when you need a specific detail. Your workspace shows documents available to your access level.
