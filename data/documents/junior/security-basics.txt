Good habits. Safer work.

Protect your account
Use a unique password and enable multi-factor authentication where available. Never share credentials or approve an unexpected sign-in request.

Handling documents
Only share documents with colleagues who have the required access. Do not copy restricted material into public tools. Report unexpected document access to the security team.

Report an incident
If you suspect a security incident, contact the security team immediately. Include what happened and when, but do not forward passwords or sensitive files.
