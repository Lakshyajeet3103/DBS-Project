# The road ahead: 2027 strategy

Our illustrative 2027 strategy focuses on a reliable product experience, clearer customer onboarding, and sustainable team growth.

## Investment priorities

Prioritize platform reliability in the first quarter. Evaluate onboarding improvements through a limited pilot before wider rollout. Review staffing needs against confirmed project commitments.

## Decision cadence

Leadership reviews progress monthly. Each initiative requires an accountable owner, a budget proposal, and a measurable outcome before approval.
